Questions

When can I get it?

Rendlune is almost ready. Subscribe on YouTube or email us and we’ll tell you the day the free trial and the $49 launch price open.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, with no time limit and no card. Every feature works; videos carry a small moving “Made with Rendlune · Free trial” mark and Masters stop at 10 minutes. Buy a license to remove both.

Do I need video editing experience?

No. You pick your lengths, type your on-screen lines and click Start Production. A Quick Start guide (in the Help menu) and “Next step” tips walk you through your first video.

What is a seamless loop?

A video that repeats a short clip so smoothly you can’t see where it restarts. Rendlune crossfades the clip’s end into its start, picture and sound, so an 8-hour video looks like one continuous scene. How invisible the loop is depends on the clip: steady, gentle motion (water, rain, fire, snow) loops best; sudden changes, people or camera moves are harder to hide.

Can I make TikToks and Instagram Reels too?

Yes. The 9:16 clips work for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, and the square clips for feeds. Rendlune writes the captions for each platform.

How long does a 12-hour video take to render?

In our testing, a 12-hour 4K Master plus eight short clips took under 8 minutes on the processor alone, without a graphics card. Rendlune renders one seamless loop and repeats it, so a longer video barely adds time. Rendering speed is hardware dependent, so treat this as an example, not a guarantee: your time will vary with your computer, your clip and the lengths and outputs you choose.

How can I make long renders faster?

Save to your fastest drive with plenty of free space. An 8-hour 4K Master is about 100–170 GB, and some SSDs slow down a lot once they’ve written their first 100 GB or so. A graphics card (NVIDIA or AMD) speeds up the Shorts and square clips.

Can I use it just to turn wide videos into Shorts and Reels?

Yes. Untick the Master and Rendlune makes only the 9:16 and 1:1 clips. It works with landscape, ultra-wide and other odd-sized footage: drag a box to choose what stays in frame.

Can I change or turn off the subscribe reminders?

Yes. Write any line you like (it doesn’t have to say “subscribe”) and choose how often it repeats on long videos: every 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or off. You can also hide the welcome or closing line for any format.

What if my clip isn’t 16:9?

It still works, vertical phone videos included. Rendlune zooms the clip to fill the frame or places it on a soft blurred copy of itself, so there are no black bars unless you want them.

What if something goes wrong?

In the app, choose Help, then Save diagnostics for support. It makes one file you can email to hello@rendlune.com so we can help faster.

Does it only work with nature clips?

It works with any short clip, but it’s made for nature and ambient footage, where gentle motion makes the loop hard to spot. Output quality also depends on your source clip: a sharp, steady, high-resolution clip gives the best 4K Master.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. AI is optional: you can type your own titles and calls to action. To let Rendlune write them, paste a key from Google (free to create at aistudio.google.com), OpenAI or Anthropic. You pay that provider directly, usually a few cents per video, at the provider’s current prices. The key stays on your PC.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

No, and there’s no account to create. Rendering happens on your PC. The only things that leave it are the license check, the one-time FFmpeg download, and, only when you use an AI button, a single frame of your clip or your prompt, sent to the AI provider you chose under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit); there’s no Mac version yet. You’ll also need enough free disk space for your videos (in our testing about 100–170 GB for an 8-hour 4K Master and 150–260 GB for 12 hours, depending on the footage, and Rendlune checks your free space before it starts). A graphics card from NVIDIA or AMD makes rendering faster, but isn’t required. No administrator rights needed to install.

Does Rendlune connect to the internet?

Only for the license check, the one-time FFmpeg download, a once-a-day check for a new version (the app tells you; nothing downloads by itself), and the AI requests you click. There’s no account to create and no tracking.

Where are my videos saved?

In C:\Rendlune Renders, one folder per project, unless you choose another folder. It’s kept outside OneDrive and Documents because the files are large.

What is FFmpeg, and why does it download?

FFmpeg is the free, widely used video tool that does the encoding. It’s a separate program with its own license, so instead of bundling it, Rendlune downloads the official release once on first start (currently about 258 MB), or uses a copy you already have.

Can I use it on my laptop and my desktop?

Yes. A license works on two computers. To move it to a new one, deactivate it on the old one (Help > License).

What happens after the 12 months of updates?

Nothing stops working: you keep using the version you have. Renew for $29 a year if you want the newer versions. Future changes to Windows or to the platforms’ rules may, in time, need a newer version.

Can I monetize the videos?

Yes. Videos made with a license are yours to publish and monetize, as long as you own, or have permission to use, the footage and any music or sound in them. Rendlune doesn’t supply or license footage. Clips you make with AI on the Generate page follow your AI provider’s terms. Each platform also decides monetization under its own rules, so check them before you rely on earnings. Rendlune can’t promise views, subscribers or income.
